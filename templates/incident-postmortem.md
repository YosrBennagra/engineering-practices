# Incident postmortem

## Summary
What happened and what was the user/system impact?

## Impact
Scope, duration and affected behavior.

## Detection
How was it detected? What should have detected it sooner?

## Timeline
Timestamped material events, decisions, mitigations and verification.

## Contributing factors
Technical and process/system conditions. Avoid stopping at “human error”.

## What worked
Detection, coordination, rollback, runbooks, safeguards.

## What slowed recovery
Missing evidence, ownership, tooling, unsafe rollback, unclear dependencies.

## Why defenses failed
Prevention, containment and detection layers.

## Actions

For each action:
- intended risk reduction;
- owner;
- priority;
- verification that it worked.

Prefer systemic changes over “be more careful”.

## Cleanup
Emergency flags, bypasses, temporary capacity or manual repair still outstanding.
