# Change plan template

## Outcome
What observable result should change?

## Current state
Relevant behavior, data ownership and constraints.

## Scope / non-goals
What is included? What is explicitly excluded?

## Assumptions and unknowns
List assumptions that could change design, estimate or rollout.

## Risks
For each major risk: impact, likelihood/evidence, mitigation.

## Increment plan
For each increment:
1. change;
2. validation;
3. compatibility;
4. rollout exposure;
5. rollback/escape path.

## Dependencies
Teams, vendors, migrations, approvals, infrastructure.

## Test / verification strategy
Link to testing detail where needed.

## Production verification
Signals and business checks.

## Cleanup
Flags, compatibility code, temporary data or follow-up debt with owner/removal condition.
